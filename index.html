What services does Simtrak provide?+

Simtrak provides HR & recruitment, graphic designing, social media management and business support services including lead generation, market research, database management, task management, customer feedback, training management, webinar & workshop management and profile building.

Who can work with Simtrak?+

Simtrak works with businesses, startups and NGOs looking for practical support across people, branding, social media and business operations.
